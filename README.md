# CodeAlpha_DataVisualization

Data Visualization project on a retail sales dataset, completed as part of the CodeAlpha Data Analytics Internship.

**Dataset:** Retail Sales Dataset (1000 transactions, 9 columns) — includes customer demographics, product category, quantity, and total spending.

**Visualizations created:**
- Bar chart — Total revenue by product category
- Bar chart — Total revenue by age group
- Pie chart — Gender distribution of customers
- Scatter plot — Age vs Total Amount spent, colored by product category
- Boxplot — Distribution of spending by product category

**Key Findings:**
- Electronics leads in total revenue, followed closely by Clothing
- Customers aged 46-55 contribute the highest total revenue
- No strong relationship between individual customer age and amount spent per transaction
- Spending amounts are broadly spread out across all product categories, not tightly clustered

**Tools used:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook
